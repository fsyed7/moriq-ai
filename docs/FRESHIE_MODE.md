# MORIQ Guide/Freshie — V1 behavior

Status: agreed V1 product direction recorded from the [PR #9 review](https://github.com/fsyed7/moriq-ai/pull/9#issuecomment-6026177279), October 6, 2026. These are requirements, not claims of implemented functionality.

MORIQ Guide/Freshie is the V1 teaching and guidance pillar for people learning architectural/project work. It should help users understand a task and its next steps, rather than assuming familiarity with every term or workflow.

## Agreed teaching scope

- Explain architectural and project terminology in context.
- Walk through workflows and the purpose of their steps.
- Explain coordination needs and provide relevant checklists.
- Identify common mistakes and how to avoid them.
- Suggest practical next steps and identify missing information needed to proceed.

## Evidence and boundaries

Use the same curated architectural knowledge and legitimate public MORIQ sources as the other pillars, with source-grounded explanations and citations. Approved private/internal sources come later. Distinguish a general workflow or suggested checklist from a verified MORIQ procedure; never invent company policies, roles, or project facts.

For code-related teaching, establish jurisdiction and edition/date and explain applicability. If context or authoritative evidence is missing, ask for it and acknowledge the limit rather than asserting compliance.

## Demonstration and evaluation

The V0.1 demo should include terminology, a workflow/coordination checklist, common mistakes, and next steps grounded in selected sources. Evaluate whether the explanation is understandable, cited, context-appropriate, and free of unsupported MORIQ claims. Specific examples, presentation details, and scoring thresholds remain to be selected; the teaching role is already agreed.

No Freshie feature, persona, onboarding policy, or UI change is implemented by this documentation correction. See [EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md).
