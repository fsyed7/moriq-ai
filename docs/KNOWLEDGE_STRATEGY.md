# MORIQ AI — Knowledge strategy

Status: proposal outline only. No sources have been approved or ingested.

## Proposed review checklist

Before adding a source, record its owner, provenance, permission to use, access classification, applicable dates, review date, and allowed audience. Define ingestion, chunking, retrieval, citations, updates, and removal only after representative source material is approved.

Keep public reference material separate from any future authorized internal material. A publicly accessible page is not automatically an approved or reusable MORIQ source. Do not infer internal knowledge from a name or domain.

## Open decisions

Source approver, ingestion frequency, embedding model, vector store, retention, access enforcement, and quality thresholds are all TBD. No MORIQ policy is established here.
