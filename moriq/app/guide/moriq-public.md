# MORIQ public record

Facts summarised from public listings and press, checked on 4 November 2026. Written in our own words. MORIQ has not confirmed these details, and they say nothing about internal standards or procedures.

## Firm profile and location
MORIQ, also published as Moriq Interiors & Design Consultants, is an architecture and interior design practice based in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, India. Houzz lists its service areas as Hyderabad and the Middle East, and ArchDaily describes its published focus as residential architecture and interior design. Listings mention the website moriq.co.in, which could not be reached when checked.
Sources: https://www.archdaily.com/office/moriq and https://www.houzz.com/pro/moriq321

## People and leadership
Houzz lists Mr. Riyaz Quraishi as Chief Consultant and Mrs. Simeen Quraishi as Associate Consultant, and describes them as a designer couple. ArchDaily credits both as lead architects. Better Interiors describes Riyaz as an architect and Simeen as an interior designer. Some press spells the surname Qureshi.
Sources: https://www.houzz.com/pro/moriq321 and http://www.betterinteriors.in/tag/moriq-interiors-design-consultants/

## Services and experience
The firm's listing offers architecture, interior architecture and design, and landscape design. It describes experience across residential, commercial, institutional and educational work, with more than a couple of hundred completed projects. It describes international work as mostly luxury homes and hotels in the Middle East. These are the firm's own statements.
Source: https://www.houzz.com/pro/moriq321

## Awards reported by the firm
The firm's Houzz profile reports IIID-MYK awards, the first Trends Excellence Awards for Architecture and Design in 2012, a National Award for excellence in design for 2016, and five Best of Houzz Design awards from 2018 to 2022. These are self-reported and were not independently checked.
Source: https://www.houzz.com/pro/moriq321

## Published projects by location
In Bahrain, published MORIQ projects are Amwaj Villa, completed 2015, and AAK Villa, completed 2016, both in Amwaj. In Hyderabad, India, they are Courtyard Villa and Villa 430, both completed 2017, the sky-high duplex apartment in Kukatpally, Apartment @ 11, a studio apartment, an apartment for a young couple, and a villa in Meenakshi Bamboos. The firm says it has completed far more work than this, so this list covers only what was found published.
Sources: the project listings in this record.

## Courtyard Villa, Hyderabad
A single-family home of about 7,500 sq ft, completed in 2017 according to ArchDaily, while Architizer lists 2018. The architects describe it as north-facing and vaastu compliant, on a linear plot with neighbouring houses to the east and west and an open plot to the south. Concrete is the listed primary material. Lead architects are Riyaz and Simeen Quraishi.
Sources: https://www.archdaily.com/913785/courtyard-villa-moriq and https://architizer.com/projects/courtyard-1/

## Villa 430, Hyderabad
A contemporary take on a joint family home on a large linear plot of 1,580 sq m, completed in 2017, with 17,006 sq ft listed by ArchDaily. Two built forms face each other across a central lawn and water body, linked by a bridge, with a separate studio apartment for the parents. Finishes combine marble, local polished limestone and concrete-finished walls. Published features include double-height master bedrooms and a projector onto the lawn wall for family gatherings.
Sources: https://www.archdaily.com/896185/villa-430-moriq and https://indiadesignid.com/villa-430-by-moriq-interiors-design-consultants/

## Amwaj Villa, Bahrain
A contemporary single-family home in Amwaj, Bahrain, completed in 2015, of about 778 sq m on a 690 sq m plot according to ArchDaily, while ek magazine reports 721 sq m. The brief emphasised interior architecture and close links between inside and outside. MORIQ is credited for architecture, interiors and landscape, and Ghumais Constructions for construction, MEP and project management.
Sources: https://www.archdaily.com/954404/amwaj-villa-moriq and https://ek-mag.com/amwaj-villa/

## AAK Villa, Bahrain
An ocean-facing home in Amwaj, Bahrain, completed in 2016, of about 977 sq m on an 809 sq m linear plot, with sea to the west and road to the east. Tall solid walls on the north and south flank neighbours, so light and air come through other openings and a courtyard. Kitchens and dining sit on the top level for the views, and the master suite faces the ocean. Creative Style is credited for interiors. Ghumais Constructions and Sirajuddin Khalid are the civil contractors.
Source: https://www.archdaily.com/801530/aak-villa-moriq

## Sky-high duplex apartment, Hyderabad
A duplex of about 710 sq m on the 29th and 30th floors in Kukatpally, also published as the Sky High Duplex. The owners first wanted fully classical interiors, and the designers proposed blending opulence with contemporary decor, which they said was faster, cheaper and easier to maintain. Social spaces and a central family lounge are layered by function across the levels. Materials include beige and black Italian marble with teak on an L-shaped metal staircase, and a sliding wooden jaali.
Sources: https://www.houzz.in/magazine/houzz-tour-contemporary-meets-classical-in-this-hyderabad-home-stsetivw-vs~75412970 and https://homeworlddesign.com/sky-high-duplex-apartment-by-moriq-studio

## Other published apartments and villas in Hyderabad
Apartment @ 11, completed in 2016, mixes modern lines and traditional elements with warm wood, patterned tile and a large mural. A studio apartment designed in 2016 was published by The Architect's Diary. An apartment for a young couple, published in 2017, uses a largely open plan despite mostly shear walls, with wooden flooring against Spanish tiles. A villa in Meenakshi Bamboos has also been published, and its details were not checked.
Sources: https://homeadore.com/?p=147582 and https://thearchitectsdiary.com/studio-apartment-in-hyderabad-by-moriq and https://indiaartndesign.com/building-narratives/ and https://homeadore.com/?p=88951

## Recurring themes in published MORIQ work
This is an interpretation of published projects, not a stated MORIQ philosophy. Common themes are linear plots organised around courts, lawns or water, strong links between inside and outside, concrete with timber and stone, neutral palettes with selective colour, and layered interiors planned around how a family gathers. Vaastu appears as a client requirement in at least one published project.
Sources: the project listings above.
