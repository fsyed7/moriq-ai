# MORIQ AI starter guide: where regulations live

AI-written pointers, checked against public sources on 4 November 2026. Some come from secondary sources. It says who governs what, and any figure given is a reported value to verify, not a design value. Always read the current official text, and confirm with the authority.

## Building permission in Hyderabad and Telangana
Telangana handles building and layout permission online through a state portal. Secondary sources report that BuildNow Telangana replaced the earlier TG-bPASS system for fresh applications on 15 December 2025, covering GHMC, HMDA, DTCP and other municipal areas, and that plans sanctioned earlier stay valid. Confirm the current portal at buildnow.telangana.gov.in before filing.
Source: https://1acre.in/research/buildnow-telangana-building-permission-online

## Telangana building rules, setbacks, height and FSI
Secondary sources cite G.O. Ms. No. 168 of 2012 as the main building rules for GHMC and HMDA areas, and their published tables disagree with each other. Do not use a website's table for setbacks, height or FSI. Get the current government order and any amendments from the Municipal Administration and Urban Development Department or the authority, and check the road width, plot size and land use for the specific site.
Source: https://www.studiomatrx.org/india/hyderabad/setbacks

## National Building Code of India
The National Building Code of India 2016, SP 7, is published by the Bureau of Indian Standards. It is a national model covering planning, structural design, fire and life safety, services and construction practice, in two volumes. It applies through local adoption, so check what the local authority has adopted. BIS also runs training on it.
Source: https://bis.gov.in/others/national-building-code/

## Indian structural standards
IS 456 covers plain and reinforced concrete. IS 875 covers design loads other than earthquake, including dead, imposed and wind loads. IS 1893 covers earthquake design criteria, and Part 1 applies to buildings. IS 13920 covers ductile detailing of reinforced concrete buildings. Check the current edition on the BIS portal, since revisions are under way. The structural engineer of record applies these.
Source: https://www.services.bis.gov.in/php/BIS_2.0/bisconnect/knowyourstandards/Indian_standards/isdetails/MjcxMzU=

## Architect registration in India
The Architects Act, 1972 set up the Council of Architecture and its register. Section 37 restricts the title and style of architect to registered persons and firms, while engineers may do their normal building work if they do not call themselves architects. A junior who is not registered should not use the title. Check the current Act and the Council's guidance on fees, conduct and appointments.
Sources: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/1690/1/A1972-20.pdf and https://www.freepressjournal.in/amp/business/council-of-architecture-warns-against-unregistered-individuals-misusing-architect-title-issues-public-advisory

## Architect fees and professional conduct in India
The Architects (Professional Conduct) Regulations, 1989 require architects to explain the Council of Architecture's conditions of engagement and scale of charges to clients, and secondary sources describe the scale as a minimum benchmark. A 2022 report says the Council's Manual of Architectural Practice replaces those documents, so check which version is current before quoting it in an appointment.
Sources: https://architectsideas.blogspot.com/2008/09/conditions-of-engagement-coa.html and https://www.thehitavada.com/Encyc/2022/8/31/Governor-Purohit-launches-COA-s-Manual-of-Architectural-Practice.html

## Eco Niwas Samhita residential energy code
Eco Niwas Samhita is the Bureau of Energy Efficiency's residential energy code, with a 2024 edition on its website. It uses the Residential Envelope Transmittance Value, RETV, to measure heat gain through walls, roofs and windows by climate zone. Read limits from the BEE document, not from websites, and check whether the state or local authority has adopted it.
Source: https://www.beeindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/BEE_ENS_2024.pdf

## IGBC and GRIHA green ratings
IGBC Green Homes, from CII's Indian Green Building Council, and GRIHA are rating systems for green buildings, generally voluntary, covering energy, water, materials and site. Check the current manual and fees, and appoint an accredited professional if the client wants a rating. Do not promise a rating level before assessment.
Source: https://www.studiomatrx.org/students/sustainable-regenerative-architecture/griha-igbc-and-indian-systems

## Hyderabad seismic zone, wind speed and climate
Secondary sources place Hyderabad in seismic Zone II under IS 1893 with a zone factor of 0.10, and give a basic wind speed of 44 m/s under IS 875 Part 3. These sites are not the standards, and some of them contain errors, so the structural engineer should confirm both from the current BIS documents. Hyderabad is generally described as composite or hot and dry with a monsoon, so check local climate data.
Source: https://infralens.in/handbook/seismic-zones and https://infralens.in/handbook/wind-speed

## Fire NOC and high-rise rules in Telangana
News reports from October 2026 say Telangana raised the residential Fire NOC height from 18 m to 24 m for fresh provisional applications from 7 October, with other building categories unchanged. Reports conflict on the details. A separate report says the building rules now class 21 m and above as high-rise. Read the official Fire Services circular and the current building rules before relying on either.
Sources: https://www.siasat.com/telangana-revises-fire-noc-guidelines-for-residential-buildings-3554836/ and https://thesouthfirst.com/telangana/telangana-raises-high-rise-threshold-to-21-metres-eases-rules-for-builders/

## RERA in Telangana
The Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 requires registration of many real estate projects, and Telangana has its own authority. General guides cite exemptions for projects at or under 500 sq m or eight apartments, and say architects may need to certify construction progress. Confirm the thresholds and forms with TG RERA, particularly for apartments and developer clients. Private single homes are usually outside it, but check.
Source: https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/rera-registration-exemption-real-estate-project-real-estate-agent.html

## Building permits in Bahrain
Bahrain handles building permits through the Benayat online portal. Owners generally apply through an engineering office licensed by the Council for Regulating the Practice of Engineering Professions, which prepares and submits documents. Files typically include architectural, structural, sewage and water drawings, the title deed, a survey certificate and owner documents. Very small works can follow a simpler route. Confirm current steps and the responsible ministry on Benayat, since ministry names have changed.
Sources: https://www.commitbiz.com/blog/benayat-in-bahrain and https://www.gdnonline.com/Details/1403410/New-services-launched

## Zoning and approvals in Bahrain
The Urban Planning and Development Authority handles planning matters, and a zoning change needs its pre-approval before a new permit application. Some project types need other bodies, such as the Supreme Council for Environment, the tourism authority for hotels, and the education and health regulators. Reported permit validity is one year from issue, renewable, and piling reportedly does not count as starting work. Confirm each point on Benayat.
Source: https://www.commitbiz.com/blog/benayat-in-bahrain

## Thermal insulation in Bahrain
Bahrain's Electricity and Water Authority guidelines describe Order 8 of 1999 as making thermal insulation compulsory for buildings, and the procedure differs above and below 2,800 sq m. EWA evaluates and approves insulation products, and it publishes the requirements. A research paper reports stricter U-value limits introduced in 2019. Get current limits and approved products from EWA before detailing a Bahrain envelope.
Sources: https://www.ewa.bh/en/Conservation/Electricity/Documents/Guidelines%20for%20Thermal%20Insulations%20Implementation.pdf and https://mdpi.com/1996-1073/18/2/367

## Fire and civil defence in Bahrain
The General Directorate of Civil Defence regulates fire safety in Bahrain, and its certificates can be obtained through the national portal. The current code text and approval steps were not confirmed from public sources, and much online material describes the UAE, which does not apply. Ask the licensed engineering office and Civil Defence what is required for each building type.
Source: https://www.bahrainthisweek.com/civil-defense-certificates-can-now-be-issued-via-bahrain-bh/

## Working in another jurisdiction
Check who may legally sign and submit drawings, whether a local licensed partner is needed, what codes the authority has adopted, and which language and units submissions require. For Gulf work, confirm the local engineering office's role and liability in the agreement. Do not assume a rule from one emirate, country or Indian state applies elsewhere.
